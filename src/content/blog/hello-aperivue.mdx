## Why Aperivue?

Radiology is at an inflection point. AI models are achieving diagnostic accuracy comparable to experienced radiologists across multiple imaging modalities, yet the gap between research and clinical practice remains wide.

Aperivue exists to bridge that gap. We build practical, evidence-based tools that radiologists can actually use — starting with structured RADS scoring and expanding into AI-assisted diagnostic workflows.

## What We're Building

**Aperivue RADS** — A unified calculator for TI-RADS, BI-RADS, Lung-RADS, and other standardized reporting systems. No more switching between different apps or reference cards.

**ScrubCode** — Our YouTube channel breaking down Medical AI papers and deep learning concepts. We believe understanding the technology is the first step to adopting it responsibly.

## What's Next

We'll be sharing our development journey, technical deep-dives into medical AI research, and updates on our tools. Follow along on [YouTube](https://youtube.com/@scrubcode) or check back here.
